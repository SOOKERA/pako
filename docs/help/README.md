# Pako help

These guides explain what Pako does today, with the names you see in the window.

1. [Installing and updating](install.md): Pako Setup or Pako Portable, the Windows warning, the licence, updates, uninstalling.
2. [Downloading](downloading.md): add links, follow your downloads, Google Drive files and folders, light or dark.
3. [Videos](videos.md): YouTube and other video sites, quality, audio only.
4. [Browser extension](browser-extension.md): add Pako to your browser, the Download button on videos, catching browser downloads, Pako Light.
5. [Links and the tray window](links-and-tray.md): pick from pasted links, and keep an eye on downloads from the taskbar.
6. [Private mode](private-mode.md): keep downloads from private windows behind a PIN or a password, encrypted on your PC.
7. [Upload](upload.md): share big files with a link, through Gofile or your own Google Drive.
8. [Direct](direct.md): send files straight to people you know, PC to PC.
9. [My devices](devices.md): pair your own PCs, send files between them without Accept, and see or start downloads on the other one.
10. [Notifications](notifications.md): pop-ups, the bell, and Do not disturb.
11. [Dashboard](dashboard.md): your Pako in numbers, kept on your PC.
12. [Help and problems](help-and-problems.md): Help and its tutorials, finding a setting, and reporting a problem with **Send report** or your own email app.
