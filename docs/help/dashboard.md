# Dashboard

Your Pako in numbers: what you downloaded, uploaded, sent and received, how fast, and with whom.

## What it counts

Every download, upload and Direct transfer is counted when it ends. Downloads that stopped with an error are counted as stopped. Downloads, uploads and Direct transfers from before the Dashboard existed were read from Pako's lists once, so they're in **All time** too, sometimes without their speed.

Everything stays on your PC. Nothing is sent anywhere.

## Period

Pick the period at the top: **Today**, **This week** (since Monday), **This month** or **All time**. It opens on **This month**. Every card follows it, except **Right now**.

## The four totals

- **Downloaded**: how much, how many files, and how many stopped with an error.
- **Uploaded**: how much, how many links, and how often people downloaded them (as Gofile last said; Google Drive doesn't share this).
- **Sent direct**: how much you sent in [Direct](direct.md), to how many people, and how much of it went straight from your PC to theirs rather than through a relay.
- **Received direct**: how much you received in Direct, from how many people, in how many transfers.

## Activity

A bar for each hour (**Today**), day or month, with what was **Downloaded**, **Uploaded**, **Sent direct** and **Received direct** stacked. Point at a bar to see the amounts.

## Speed

The **Average** speed while things were moving, and **Up to the top speed** reached, for **Downloads**, **Uploads** or **Direct** (pick one at the top right of the card). The highest point is marked. Point at the chart for the numbers.

## Which way is fastest

- **Sending files**: the average upload speed by the way you sent them: **Gofile link**, **Google Drive link** and **Direct**.
- **Getting files**: the average download speed by where files came from: **Google Drive**, **Videos**, **Other sites**, **Received in Direct**, and **Earlier downloads** (older ones whose source isn't known).

Each row shows how many, how much, and the top speed. **Fastest** marks the quickest when at least two can be compared.

## Your people

Who you exchanged the most with in Direct: what you sent to and received from each person, and the fastest speed each way. Click a person to open them in Direct. Someone you removed shows as **Removed contact**.

## Where files came from

Everything you downloaded or received, shared out by source, and the **Top sites** you downloaded from.

## Records

- **Fastest download**: the top speed, with the file and the day.
- **Biggest file**: the biggest single file you downloaded or received, with its name and the day.
- **Busiest day**: the day the most moved, all kinds together.

## Right now

How fast Pako downloads, next to everything your PC receives and sends (**Whole PC · download** and **Whole PC · upload**), over the **Last hour** or the **Last 24 hours**. It fills in while Pako is open.

## Save or reset

The gear at the top right has:

- **Save the details as a CSV file…**: one line per transfer, in a file that opens in Excel. The details of the last 12 months or so are kept; older ones only count in the totals.
- **Reset statistics…**: every number on the Dashboard starts again from zero, including the speed history. Your files, downloads, uploads and contacts stay as they are. This can't be undone.
